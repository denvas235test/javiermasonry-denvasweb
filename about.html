About Javier Masonry Service

Javier Masonry Service is a brick and stone masonry business based in Katy, Texas. Javier builds and repairs brickwork for homeowners in Katy, Cypress, Tomball and Houston.

The work ranges from small repairs and garden borders to full outdoor kitchens. On bigger jobs he handles the whole build, from the structure to the finishing details, so there is one person to talk to from start to finish.

If you are planning a project, call or send your details and Javier will get back to you.
